How it works

Ten dials the dev sets. Every one of them yours.

Pick the pair, the tax, what it buys and who gets paid by which rule. Then any of six more. Signed at launch, run by the engine every epoch, published as a ledger.

1

The pair

Any of 600+ quote assets: SOL, USDC, gold, tokenized stocks, staked SOL, memes. Buyers can pay in SOL for any of them; Jupiter routes the swap.

2

The tax

0 to 5% on every transfer, in steps of 0.01%. Your number: the program locks it on the curve, and the platform never changes it after. The one token whose rate may change after graduation is $WGMI itself.

3

What it buys

Anything Jupiter routes, picked by name: gold, staked SOL, BTC, a stock, the quote, or the token itself. Or burn it. Or fund the treasury. Any split, up to ten sinks.

4

Who gets paid, by which rule

Pro-rata, holding-time weighted, never-sold bonus or a balance-weighted lottery, each with an optional minimum holding. Splitting a wallet never raises its payout.

Six more. Off by default, yours at launch.

5

Buy-tax refund

Buyers get their tax back at the next epoch, grossed up so it arrives whole. Only sells pay.

6

Later recipe

A second split takes over at graduation, at a holder count or at a market cap. Signed before launch, checked every epoch, never reversed.

7

Minimum holding age

Tokens count only after 1 hour to 7 days in the wallet, per lot. A sniper who flips inside the wait feeds the pot.

8

Jackpot

A lottery that draws every N epochs. Between draws the pot piles up, with a countdown on the token page.

9

Locked team share

A slice locked by Raydium's program at launch, with a cliff and a linear unlock after graduation. You decide whether it earns payouts.

10

Referral share

Up to 1% of every curve trade to whoever brought the buyer, paid by the program. Every visitor of the token page gets a share-and-earn link.

1 sweep the tax→2 refund the buys→3 convert it→4 snapshot holders→5 apply the rule→6 pay→7 publish the ledger

Every epoch, hourly if you like. Payouts too small to deliver go to the other holders paid that epoch; a pot too small to convert waits for the next. Nothing is rounded away.

Why launch here

Six reasons, all of them on-chain.

The cheapest a LaunchLab curve can be, and every option in the dev's hands. Each one is readable on-chain or in Raydium's docs.

The cheapest a LaunchLab curve can be.

0.75% all-in on the WGMI account. The platform takes 0%; only Raydium's 0.25% and the dev's own fee remain. Two more accounts bring it to 0.50% or 0.25%.

Your fees stay yours.

The 0.5% creator fee accrues to the dev's own on-chain vault, and only they can claim it. After graduation, 0.42 of every pool swap's 1.00% is forwarded to the dev.

Any pair, any payout asset.

600+ quote assets and any payout token Jupiter routes, both by name in the form. Tax 0 to 5%. Up to ten sinks: holders, treasury, creator, burn, in any split. Set once, run every epoch.

Rules that cannot be gamed.

Pro-rata, holding-time weighted, never-sold bonus or a balance-weighted lottery, each with a minimum holding and a minimum holding age. Splitting a wallet never raises its payout; rules that reward wash trading are refused.

Buys tax-free, sells pay.

The buy tax refunded within the hour, and a recipe that changes on a signed schedule.

The pool deepens with every trade.

At graduation half of the LP is burned and half locked, so half of every swap's liquidity fee stays in the pool forever. Nobody can pull it.

Also true: the mint authority is revoked at creation, and every payout epoch is published as a ledger with its snapshot, allocations and transaction signatures, so anyone can recompute it.
